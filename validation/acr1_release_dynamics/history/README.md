# Superseded collision-surface pilot

These native MuJoCo XML/JSON/CSV records and the accompanying matrix preserve the initial exploratory surface subset. They are excluded from the adopted 42-run campaign and its quantitative conclusions. The adopted model adds complete pallet rectangular members, including the grip end faces only 0.5 mm below the payload, and fixed cap-guide surfaces.

The pilot XML files preserve their collision geometry. Use the current SOURCE_REPLAY.json/NPZ histories and the recorded case/solver settings to interpret their force boundary. Console duplicates and a transient diagnostic-script traceback were omitted from the publication package; the scientific model/state records are retained here.
