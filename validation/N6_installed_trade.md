# N6: installed-system break-even requirements

Adityavardhan Mishra · 25 September 2026

Freeze before calculation. Compare the same twelve 4 kg propulsionless payloads
and same host mission. Sweep incremental installed cell mass above a competent
conventional deployer at 0/.25/.5/1/2 kg per payload, additional shared burden
0/2/5/10 kg, aggregate host delta-v avoided 0/5/20/50/100 m/s, and host Isp
60/220/320 s. Retained dry mass is 300 kg plus payloads plus incremental mass;
conventional installed deployer mass is included within the 300 kg assumed
baseline. Do not call the matrix measured hardware or a mission simulation.
Compare wet masses with the rocket equation, counting incremental dry mass's
own propellant burden for common host delta-v 0/100/500 m/s. Report the exact
break-even avoided delta-v; allow negative net benefit and retain every case.
No component can be zeroed simply because it has not been designed. Create an
accounting ledger with explicit unknowns; no complete installed mass or cost
claim may be made until those entries are measured or quoted.

Independent check: algebraic break-even wet masses agree within 1e-8 kg; zero
incremental mass/zero saving gives exactly zero benefit; increasing incremental
mass must never improve net benefit. Cost, reliability and host availability
remain separate unclosed evidence. Jam isolation is deterministic topology, not
an inferred probability of mission success.
