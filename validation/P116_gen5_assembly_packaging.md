# P116 — Gen5 reference assembly and packaging screen

**Run:** 9 October 2026. **Disposition:** a named 20-instance review STEP is released, and its stated side-fed reference arrangement **fails** geometric interference. It is not a flight assembly, motion study or an approved interface.

The [assembly generator](../cad/build_review_assembly.py) imports the eight Gen5 STEP parts, places the interface, track, stator, sled, brake and enclosure, adds two magazine cassettes and twelve 3U payload proxies, and exports a named [assembly STEP](../cad/step/gen5/VOLLEY_Review_Assembly_Gen5.step). The [machine-readable report](../cad/REVIEW_ASSEMBLY.json) pins input hashes, placements and exact-solid overlap. A repeat export gave the same SHA-256 after the STEP header was normalized. The [cross-section](../figures/gen5_packaging_section.svg) draws the declared layout, not a manufacturing drawing.

FreeCAD 1.0 also imports the eight solids into a [native FCStd document](../cad/native/Gen5_Review.FCStd), re-exports [eight part STEP files](../cad/step/freecad_gen5/) and a [20-instance assembly STEP](../cad/step/gen5/VOLLEY_Review_Assembly_FreeCAD_Gen5.step), and reads the export back as a valid B-rep. Its independent exact-solid intersection gives the same 32,915 mm³ on each side; software version, hashes and limits are in the [FreeCAD export JSON](../cad/FREECAD_EXPORT.json) and the [CAD review PDF](../cad/GEN5_CAD_REVIEW.pdf). The imported parts are editable in placement and grouping but have no native FreeCAD parametric feature history.

For this side-fed reference, each 166 mm-wide cassette is placed against the internal side face of the 530 mm-wide, 2 mm-skin enclosure. Internal clear width is **526 mm**. Two cassettes plus the 205 mm-wide track need **537 mm before any clearance**, an **11 mm shortfall**. At the declared 200 mm cassette x offset and z=0..690 mm, exact B-rep intersections find **32,915 mm³ track/cassette overlap on each side**. The cassettes also have zero side-wall clearance. The reference placement therefore does not pass fit. A different vertical feed, cutout or larger enclosure could alter the outcome, but none is modeled or cleared here.

This assembly does not resolve feeder motion, retention, stator end turns, wiring, fasteners, tolerance stacks, thermal growth, structural load paths or an actual CubeSat/host ICD. STEP proxy volumes must not be turned into the 126.6 kg model rollup: materials, fill, components and several masses use different assumptions. The current 3U design should be described as an evaluated computational configuration **with a packaging defect**, not a completed manufacturable product.

Reproduce with `python3 cad/build_review_assembly.py` after `cad/build_gen5.py`; the part hashes in the report allow a reviewer to establish which solids were assembled. Run `python3 cad/plot_review_packaging.py` to rebuild the section figure.
