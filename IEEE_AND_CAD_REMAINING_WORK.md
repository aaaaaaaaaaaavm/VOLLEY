## Three different decisions

| Decision | Defensible release criterion |
|:--|:--|
| **College computational-study submission** | A fixed question, one traceable configuration, reproducible reported cases, correct citations, explicit failed criteria, limitations, report and reviewed slides. A negative feasibility result can complete the *study*. The university's actual format and authorship approval still govern submission. |
| **IEEE paper submission** | A narrow original contribution relative to verified full-text prior art; defensible models and numerical checks for every headline result; fair matched comparator; uncertainty and convergence; accessible reproducibility package; venue-specific length, ethics, references and formatting. Peer review and acceptance are external. |
| **Engineering build/flight release** | A geometrically feasible design, controlled drawings and installed BOM, named interfaces and payload, calibrated physical tests, qualification and provider acceptance. Computation and literature cannot substitute for these. |

The present 3U configuration fails its chosen installed-mass screen (10.547 kg per 3U versus 2 kg per 3U) and its side-fed CAD fit (537 mm parts in 526 mm clear width; exact-solid overlap of 32,915 mm³ per side). The historical gross energy arithmetic now closes, while its source and components remain unverified. No tested twelve-shot host campaign closes. These are results to report, not values to hide or thresholds to move. A paper can publish a bounded negative design study; it cannot call this a completed commercial deployer.
