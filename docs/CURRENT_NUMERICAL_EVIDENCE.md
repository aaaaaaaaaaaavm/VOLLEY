### RVC01: FM07-R6 candidate

The [RVC01 source and CAD capture](../validation/feeder_rotary_clearance/README.md) records actual **FreeCAD 1.0.0 / OCC 7.8.1** execution in `CONTINUOUS_ROTARY_CLEARANCE.json` and `DELTA_AND_EXPORT.json`. All four nominal opening/closing angle intervals for 1U/3U pass the adaptive B-rep-distance/rigid-motion screen; the smallest guarded conservative lower bound is **0.009884598 mm**. Eight full native assembly exports and ten STEP files (eight assemblies, two gates) pass the separate CadQuery readback in `INDEPENDENT_STEP.json`. CadQuery uses a separate importer with OpenCascade; this is not a different-kernel check.

R6 raises the gate panel's lower edge by 0.25 mm and removes material to resolve the R5 near-closed gate/deck surface contact. The R5 witness has zero volumetric intersection at the checked near-closed angles; it must not be relabeled a demonstrated volumetric collision. RVC01 supplies a nominal numerical clearance bound, not a measured clearance, tolerance allowance or certified kernel error enclosure. This separate revision does not confer R6 contact convergence, loaded-fit acceptance, feeder selection or qualification.
