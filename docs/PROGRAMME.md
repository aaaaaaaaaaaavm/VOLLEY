# VOLLEY project structure

| Repository | Contents |
|:--|:--|
| [VOLLEY](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/VOLLEY) | Project overview, generation history, integrated engineering studies, CAD and website |
| [VOLLEY Labs](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/VOLLEY-lab) | Complete research catalogue, alternatives, rejected concepts and successor experiments |
| [VOLLEY Thesis](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/VOLLEY-thesis) | Independently maintained report, presentation and local supporting engineering record |
| [VOLLEY Paper](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/VOLLEY-paper) | Independently maintained manuscript, methods, figures and reproduction data |

Each study and academic edition names its configuration, source and results. Shared source identities are checked explicitly; a newer result in one repository does not silently revise another repository's manuscript or captured evidence.

Native CAD, STEP, solver captures and published documents retain their release identities. Superseded concepts remain in the [research archive](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/VOLLEY-lab/tree/main/archive); the [generation record](generations/README.md) preserves the design evolution. [File and archive map](REPOSITORY_MAP.md).
