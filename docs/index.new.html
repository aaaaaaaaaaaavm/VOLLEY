01 · the job

A deployer is already changing the orbit. The question is whether that change can be useful.

The host owns coarse orbital state and resources. The deployment system owns the relative release condition. The spacecraft owns everything after contact ends. VOLLEY is the trade between those three.

host

Place and recover

Navigation, attitude authority, permitted manoeuvres, recoil recovery and campaign reserves stay in host accounting.

release

Control the departure

Relative Δv, direction, timing, retention, tip-off and repeatability are the deployment-system job.

spacecraft

Then it is on its own

No continuing stationkeeping, collision avoidance or formation control is implied after separation.

02 · the machine

Five years of deleting assumptions.

The mission idea survived. The mechanism did not. The current redesign is deliberately not rendered until architecture selection is earned.

2021

Coilgun on a free-flyer

The project began by naming the distribution problem. In 2023 the host changed to a spent upper stage; by mid-2025 the coilgun gave way to a linear synchronous motor because commanded velocity, not simply ejection, was the product.

2023 ADR-002

POEM reframed the mission around a retained, controlled upper stage rather than a dedicated free-flyer.
