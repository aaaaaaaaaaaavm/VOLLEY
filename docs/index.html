VOLLEY · engineering study

Programmable spacecraft departure is the research goal

Gen6 is in development. No design has been selected, built or validated. VOLLEY is investigating a reusable launcher that loads CubeSats in sequence and commands each relative release speed.

The machine VOLLEY needs

One shared release path and a loading mechanism should serve a manifest. The proposed speed range and compatibility with ordinary CubeSats remain open engineering questions.

Earlier studies

The independent motor-charged spring-cell bank and the long gas guide are unselected studies. Their simulations do not prove a shared feeder, a complete mission benefit or payload qualification.

Evidence boundary

The Gen5 electromagnetic baseline is calculated, not physically measured. A two-payload timing screen cannot set the product maximum. Six later twelve-payload screens failed full delivery under their stated assumptions.

Next decision

Compare shared electromagnetic and other drive concepts with the same payload, host, full manifest, installed mass, volume and failure assumptions. Then test the selected components and repeated releases.
