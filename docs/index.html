Gen6 / Selected feeder

Guided transfer. Independent payload custody.

FM08-R3 uses a guided lead-screw pusher to transfer passive 1U and 3U spacecraft from individually restrained fixed cassettes into one indexed receiver. Fixed 1U spacers remain inside their cassettes. The selected academic architecture uses capped transfer channels, far-side receiver stops and a reversible pusher stroke.

01 / Design decision

Gen5 evaluation.

The reference arrangement is a reproducible design study. It is not a qualified deployer or an accepted final configuration. These values separate predictions from exact-solid geometry checks.

Finite force screen
12.448 m/s

Ideal phase, finite 3-D geometry; circuit losses omitted. The historical 16.029 m/s periodic-force result is challenged.
Independent 2-D FEM
1.082 kJ

Ideal in-plane work on a 1 mm mesh. It corroborates finite-stator decline within the stated model limits.
Installed mass screen
10.55 kg / 3U

Modeled dry mass per customer; 76% above an approximate 6 kg canister comparator.
Reference CAD fit
11 mm short

The side cassettes exceed the stated inner width by 11 mm. An expanded all-leaf audit finds 17 intersecting pairs, including sled/track and sled/stator.
02 / System resource comparison

Installed mass and host propellant.

In a matched one-event reference, ideal finite-force Gen5 uses 1.866 kg less host fuel than a spring class when both carry 10 kg. Its modeled device is 54.562 kg heavier. If fuel is hypothetically resized for only that event and equal reserve, Gen5 still carries 52.717 kg more device plus fuel.

At the same ideal speed, that narrow parity screen requires about 52.630 kg less Gen5 device dry mass. It is a redesign target, not a selected solution, complete twelve-shot mission or validated motor rating.

04 / Separate development study

Gen6: mixed-size deployment and reusable handling.

The reference service carries two passive 1U and two passive 3U spacecraft on a generic maneuvering orbital-transfer host near 500 km sun-synchronous orbit. Releases occur within one orbital period; all terminal position and velocity targets share one epoch 24 hours after the final release. Host-assisted spring deployment uses the same comparison boundary.

FM07-R5 defines cassette transfer, captive receiver pins, exit-gate opening and empty-carrier reset for dedicated 1U and 3U handling coupons. Its nominal checks cover 46 cycle poses, 34 intermediate gate angles and 40 continuous linear sweeps. Twenty assembly STEP files and 74 individual parts pass independent exchange inspection.

These are nominal geometry results. Loaded fits, installed actuation and the seven-concept selection gates remain engineering requirements. FCE12 separately evaluates contact representation and final-state sensitivity on FM06 geometry. A 1 km/s-class release remains a long-range research objective.
