# Gen5 structural and environmental disposition

The evaluated 20-instance Gen5 geometry fails the exact-solid assembly census.
No same-revision installed launch/release finite-element model, complete load path,
mesh-converged stress/mode result or vibration qualification is available. Historical
isolated chassis, plate and tube studies remain limited to their own geometry,
boundary conditions and loads; their stress margins cannot qualify this assembly.

The 5/10/25 g values selected for future sensitivity studies are assumed load cases.
They are neither payload allowables nor an approved launch-provider environment.
Random-vibration RMS acceleration cannot be converted into a universal quasi-static
strength limit by applying a three-sigma multiplier.

A future corrected revision requires explicit rail/bearing, stator/backstop, adapter,
restraint and brake load paths; joint stiffness and preload; payload CG/inertia;
provider launch/vibration/shock bands; thermal expansion; and modal/transient cases
with convergence and uncertainties. No qualification or positive structural
capability is claimed for the frozen Gen5 assembly.
