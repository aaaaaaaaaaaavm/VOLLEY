machine evolution

The mission survived. The mechanisms kept losing arguments.

VOLLEY began in 2021 around a coilgun. In 2023 the host became a spent upper stage. By mid-2025 the mechanism became a linear synchronous motor. In 2026 the stage itself became part of the machine. The current architecture is open again.
