# Project and evidence boundary

VOLLEY is independently controlled. Its design, calculations, CAD, reported evidence, tests and completion gates do not depend on the separate BOLLEY side project. Work may be reused outward from VOLLEY; a side-project result does not establish a VOLLEY result.

The evaluated Gen5 configuration retains its adverse findings. Gen6 concepts remain separately identified until their own requirements, geometry, installed resources and failure evidence justify a selection. Historical cross-project studies remain dated records rather than current dependencies or inherited validation.

A VOLLEY release must build and explain its conclusions from the local declared inputs and evidence. Its report, paper and presentation distinguish numerical agreement, model credibility, measured behavior and provider acceptance. No side-project hardware milestone is required to complete the VOLLEY academic submission.
