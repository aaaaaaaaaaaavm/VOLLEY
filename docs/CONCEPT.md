# Commanded electromagnetic deployment

**Current reference: VOLLEY-G5-ACR1.** VOLLEY combines independently restrained storage and guided transfer with a retained electromagnetic launch sled. The host supplies its own maneuvers; launch impulse is one contribution to the final payload state.

Twelve 4 kg 3U envelope surrogates occupy two fixed six-level banks. A lead-screw pusher feeds the selected satellite to a captive vertical cradle. A reusable pallet docks to the sled, the cradle withdraws, and independent custody/path/source proofs enable acceleration. The pallet stays on the carrier through release, active arrest and empty return.

The evaluated command range is 1–3 m/s over 1.300 m powered travel. The same-target mission includes installed mass, host propellant and recovery. Its preplanned cases find no propellant or initial wet-mass advantage over the ideal ground-pretrimmed spring comparator; no unique inflight-retasking benefit is established by preknown synthetic targets.
