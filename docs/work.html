Current status

Define the operating envelope

Specify payload classes, loading cadence, lower and upper commanded speeds, allowable contact loads, host envelope, energy and pointing resources.

Compare complete systems

Use one host and manifest for shared electromagnetic and other drive paths, banks, historical gas and spring studies, and competent conventional deployment. Count empty spent hardware after every shot.

Falsify early

Test feeder indexing and jam recovery, retention, drive force, release repeatability, tip-off and catcher behaviour on instrumented hardware before detailed flight packaging.

Protect the evidence trail

Historical model outputs and failed cases remain available with their original configurations. Earlier architecture selection language is withdrawn.
