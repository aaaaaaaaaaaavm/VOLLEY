02 / Necessary mass bound

A perfect motor alone cannot close this trade.

In the one-event reference, even zero host propellant use leaves Gen5 needing a device at or below 74.659 kg to tie the spring-class device plus fuel. The current model is 126.562 kg. This bound says nothing about an achievable new motor or a complete mission.

02b / Define the delivered state

Start with orbital accuracy and payload loads.

The P124 requirements screen turns a declared 450 km orbital-state target into an ideal tangential velocity margin. At a 1.3 m stroke, 1 km/s implies about 39,220 g and 2 MJ of payload kinetic energy for an ordinary 4 kg 3U. That high-end objective is separate research; a first prototype needs a named mission, allowed load and measured command band.
