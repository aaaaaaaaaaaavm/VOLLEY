What the evidence can support

ProvenanceS12 study

S4 is a bounded two-payload screen

Its best sampled relative speed near 4.57 m/s applies to a chosen host, targets, timing and idealized model. It does not define VOLLEY's product speed ceiling.

S12 did not complete the manifest

Six twelve-payload finite-burn cases failed complete delivery; the best tested case delivered five. This is a bounded model result, not a universal impossibility proof.

Payload limits are specific

Random vibration Grms, static acceleration and short shock pulses are different loads. No general CubeSat g rating qualifies a high-speed guided push.

Engineering closure

A named payload, provider interface, matched full installed budget, uncertainty model and calibrated multi-shot hardware evidence are still required.
