Method boundary

Read each result in its declared model.

Geometry and mechanisms

Native solid geometry, nominal motion, interface retention and declared analytical sizing. Reference actuator envelopes and allocations are identified in the source; manufacturing and instrumented hardware correlation follow.

Motor and release

Finite geometry and coupled computational source/motion models. The 1–3 m/s command band is the supported study range; no hardware rating or flight capability is asserted.

Orbital delivery

Fixed-axis J2 point masses, synthetic preplanned full-state targets, constant inertial host force and Isp 220 s. Drag, higher gravity, attitude/plume allocation, navigation and finite exit geometry are outside the mission model.

Numerical agreement

Independent replay and step refinement assess numerical consistency. They do not establish physical delivery accuracy, measured reliability or payload compatibility.

Installed allocation

149.55672 kg computed CAD mass + 7 kg power/control allowance = 156.55672 kg retained device allocation; + 48 kg payloads = 204.55672 kg loaded device. The generic wet host is 500 kg including its 20 kg fuel once, so the mission begins at 704.55672 kg.
