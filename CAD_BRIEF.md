# VOLLEY-G5-ACR1 CAD and mechanism

The current integrated reference contains twelve 4 kg 3U envelope surrogates in two six-level banks. Guided lead-screw feed, a vertical captive cradle, one retained pallet and a reusable electromagnetic sled form the checked mechanism. The powered travel is 1.300 m; supported modeled commands are 1–3 m/s.
