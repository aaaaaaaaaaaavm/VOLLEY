# VOLLEY current reference

**VOLLEY-G5-ACR1**, captured 24–25 October 2026, is the integrated academic reference: twelve 4 kg 3U envelope surrogates, two six-cell banks, guided lead-screw feeding, captive cradle/pallet transfer and retained electromagnetic sled.

The supported modeled commands are 1, 2 and 3 m/s over 1.300 m powered travel. Eight native/STEP states and 154 accepted installed-motion/refinement cases establish the declared nominal geometry boundary. Eighteen source/inductance sensitivity cases support the command range. Represented CAD dry mass is 149.556720 kg; mission device mass is 156.556720 kg including the separate 7 kg electrical/control allowance.

The twelve-release synthetic same-target mission uses a nominal 500 km SSO maneuvering host and a common evaluation epoch 24 h after the last release. Nine native Orekit replays support numerical agreement under shared J2 assumptions. The tested preplanned cases show no propellant or initial wet-mass advantage over the ideal ground-pretrimmed spring comparator.
