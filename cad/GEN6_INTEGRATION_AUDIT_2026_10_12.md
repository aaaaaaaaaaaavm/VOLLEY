# VOLLEY CAD interference and integration audit — 12 October 2026

**Status:** engineering finding, not a CAD release. Exact-solid checks use the saved FreeCAD 1.0 B-reps and exported STEP. Gen5 remains the fixed evaluated academic configuration; Gen6 R1 is a separate unselected geometry experiment. All distances are millimetres and intersections are cubic millimetres. A zero nominal solid intersection does not establish launch, thermal, tolerance, contact or provider compatibility.

## Audit boundary and method

The [Gen5/R1 pair census](GEN5_FULL_INTERFERENCE_AUDIT_2026_10_12.json) checks every leaf-solid pair in the native 20-instance Gen5 and 21-instance widened R1 review documents, excluding each aggregate assembly shape so parts are not compared against a duplicate of themselves. The [Gen6 cross-assembly census](gen6_integration_r1/release/Original_Cross_Assembly_Interference.json) combines each original one-cell feeder state with both first-article candidates. [Gen6 integrated R1](gen6_integration_r1/README.md) is a separately saved corrective geometry and its [STEP verification](gen6_integration_r1/release/Gen6_Integrated_R1_STEP_Verification.json) independently checks exported solids.

## Configuration consequence

CAD-02–04 add failures to the already adverse Gen5 CAD record; they do not disappear because R1 clears cassette width. Any positive Gen5 design claim would need a new controlled revision and dependent force, mass, structural, interface and mission reruns. CAD-05 demonstrates a narrower correction: the combined *one-cell* Gen6 R1 clears sampled collisions and R2 removes the nominal 10 mm unsupported handoff gap with an extending support proxy. Both revisions retain unresolved sequencing, contact and fault gates. It is not a selected feeder, a four-cell system, or a verified power-off mechanism. The authoritative status and next actions are maintained in AVM Work Index **BLACK BOOK FINAL** (BB-14, BB-19, BB-54–60); this report is the technical evidence, not a second task list.

The [separate R2 native/STEP package](gen6_integration_r2/README.md) contains the nominal bridge-continuity change and readback. The [four-cell R0 package](gen6_four_cell_r0/README.md) compares staggered and paired mixed-size placements. The latter mitigates the short ideal acceleration path; neither is a released feeder.
